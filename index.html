    From first step
    to finished packet.

    Give yourself time to gather documents and request a recommendation. Use this checklist before you submit.

    01 / GATHER

    Bring the pieces together.

    Complete the six sections and include the required documents. Explain pending aid or missing documents in the application.

    Upload a digital photo of the applicant directly inside the online application — everything is delivered together in one submission.

      02 / REVIEW
