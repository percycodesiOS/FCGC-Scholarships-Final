Cover letter page 1

Dear 2027 Scholarship Applicant, Thank you for your interest in the Fox Chapel Golf Club Scholarship Foundation's 2027 scholarship program. We support post-secondary education for Club employees, children of employees, and caddies through the generous donations of Club members. Awards are based on academic performance, aptitude tests (e.g., ACT and SAT), school, community and work involvement, financial need, the personal essay, recommendations, and any interview. Scholarships are awarded for one year; eligible applicants may reapply in subsequent years. This is a scholarship program, not an employee bonus. Application Requirements and Deadline To be eligible to apply, employees must have commenced employment at the Club before January 1, 2027, and must have completed a minimum of 160 hours of work since their start date. Caddies must have completed at least 15 rounds (preferably 20) during 2026 and be clearly trending toward at least 15 rounds (preferably 20) in 2027. All applicants must be enrolled at a qualified school for the Fall 2027 semester. Submit the completed application and required materials by Friday, June 18, 2027, at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time. Late applications will not be considered. Missing documents will invalidate the application unless an explanation is included with the submission. The committee will not follow up to obtain missing documents. Checklist of Required Documents Your application will be considered complete if it includes the following: • Complete the 2027 application digitally. Handwritten applications and previous years' application forms are not eligible. • One letter of recommendation from a person outside Fox Chapel Golf Club. Letters from FCGC personnel do not qualify. Provide the recommender's name, address and phone number on the application. • Personal essay (1-2 pages), in your own words: describe your educational and career goals, how the scholarship would help, and significant experiences that shaped your aspirations. • First two pages of your FAFSA summary, including your Student Aid Index. If you did not submit the FAFSA, explain why. • School acceptance letter or financial aid offer letter; include a school financial aid estimate if that is the available aid documentation. Explain any unavailable documents. • Complete the full-year school cost matrix, list each scholarship and grant by name and amount, and show the total cost, total scholarships and grants, and amount not funded. • Email a digital photo of the applicant to wparks@foxchapelgolfclub.com, as directed on the application.

Cover letter page 2

Tips for a Strong Application A strong application gives the committee a clear understanding of your achievements, financial circumstances and goals. Please: • Ensure that all sections of the application are completed thoroughly. • Provide specific details about your academic achievements, work experience, and community involvement. • Write the personal essay in your own words so the committee can understand your motivations and aspirations. • Double-check your application for accuracy and completeness before submission. Financial Need Financial need is an important consideration. Some families may not qualify for federal financial aid but still face substantial educational costs. The Student Aid Index is a guideline in evaluating financial need. Please explain your circumstances clearly and accurately. Use your school's estimated cost for one academic year, including tuition, fees, room and board, and related expenses such as books and transportation. List all scholarships and grants with the name and amount of each, total the scholarships and grants, and show the remaining amount not funded. Attach the supporting documents listed above and explain any pending aid, missing documents or special circumstances. Next Steps After the deadline, the scholarship committee will review the applications. Selected applicants may be invited to an interview to discuss their motivations, character and future goals. Submitting an application does not guarantee an interview or a scholarship. An interview does not guarantee a scholarship. For questions, call the Club Front Office so your call can be directed appropriately, or email scholarshipfoundation@foxchapelgolfclub.com. Thank you for your interest. The Foundation wishes you well in your educational pursuits. Sincerely, The Fox Chapel Golf Club Scholarship Foundation The official registration and financial information of the Fox Chapel Golf Club Scholarship Foundation may be obtained from the Pennsylvania Department of State by calling toll free, within Pennsylvania, 1-800-732-0999. Registration does not imply endorsement.
