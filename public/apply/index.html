Cover letter page 1

Fox Chapel Golf Club Scholarship Foundation, Inc. 426 Fox Chapel Road ∙ Pittsburgh, PA 15238 (412) 967-9081 Dear Scholarship Applicant, Thank you for your interest in applying for the Fox Chapel Golf Club Foundation scholarship. We are proud to support the educational goals of club employees, children of employees, and caddies, through this program. The Foundation's mission is to assist deserving individuals in pursuing post-secondary education by providing financial support based on academic performance, aptitude tests (e.g. ACT, SAT, etc.), recommendations, financial need, and personal interviews. The scholarships granted are for a one-year duration, and applicants are encouraged to reapply in subsequent years if they continue to meet the eligibility requirements. The scholarships are made possible through the generous donations of Fox Chapel Golf Club members. This is a scholarship program, not an employee bonus. To ensure a smooth application process, we would like to highlight the following important information: Application Requirements and Deadline To be eligible to apply: • Employees must have commenced employment at the Club before January 1, 2027, and must have completed a minimum of 160 hours of work since their start date. • Caddies must have completed a minimum of 15 rounds (preferably 20) during the preceding year and be clearly trending toward completing at least 15 rounds (preferably 20) in the current year. • All applicants must be enrolled at a qualified school for the Fall 2027 semester. Please submit your completed application, including all required materials, by Friday, June 18, 2027, at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time. Late submissions will not be considered. Missing documents will nullify the application unless explained. We will not follow up. We strongly encourage you to begin the process early to ensure that all materials are submitted on time. Checklist of Required Documents Your application will be considered complete if it includes the following: • 2027 application form completed on the Foundation website (handwritten applications will not be accepted). Previous years' application forms will not be accepted. • One letter of recommendation from a person outside Fox Chapel Golf Club. Letters from FCGC personnel do not qualify. • Personal essay (1-2 pages): Your essay should describe your educational and career goals, how this scholarship will help you achieve them, and any significant experiences that have shaped your aspirations. • First two pages of the FAFSA summary, including the Student Aid Index. If you did not submit the FAFSA, explain why. • School acceptance letter or financial aid offer letter. Explain any unavailable documents. • Before submitting your application, upload a digital photo of yourself in the "Upload applicant photo" section of the online form.

Cover letter page 2

Tips for a Strong Application The scholarship committee values a complete and well-rounded application that provides a clear understanding of your academic performance, extracurricular activities, financial needs, and future goals. To help you submit a strong application, please consider the following: • Ensure that all sections of the application are completed thoroughly. • Provide specific details about your academic achievements, work experience, and community involvement. • When writing your personal essay, aim to give the committee a sense of who you are, your motivations, and what drives your aspirations. • Double-check your application for accuracy and completeness before submission. Financial Need While financial need is an important consideration, the Foundation recognizes that financial circumstances vary widely among applicants. Some families may not qualify for federal financial aid yet still face significant financial burdens due to the rising cost of education. Our goal is to understand the context of each applicant’s financial circumstances to ensure that the scholarship is awarded to those who will benefit most from this support. Please be as detailed and transparent as possible to help us in our decision-making process. In the Financial Information section, use your school's estimate of costs for the 2027-2028 school year and list the name and amount of each scholarship or grant already awarded for that year. Explain pending aid or unavailable documents. The Student Aid Index is a guideline in evaluating financial need. Next Steps Once the application deadline has passed, the scholarship committee will review all submissions. Finalists will be invited to a brief interview with the committee, during which we hope to learn more about your motivations, character, and future goals. We appreciate the time and effort you put into your application. Please note that the submission of an application does not guarantee an interview or a scholarship. An interview does not guarantee a scholarship. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please feel free to call the Club Front Office or email scholarshipfoundation@foxchapelgolfclub.com. The Foundation appreciates your interest and wishes you well in your future endeavors. Sincerely, The Fox Chapel Golf Club Scholarship Foundation The official registration and financial information of the Fox Chapel Golf Club Scholarship Foundation may be obtained from the Pennsylvania Department of State by calling toll free, within Pennsylvania, 1-800-732-0999. Registration does not imply endorsement.
