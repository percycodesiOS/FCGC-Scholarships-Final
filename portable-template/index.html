AN OPPORTUNITY TO GROW

For the people who
make our Club community.

Through the generosity of Club members, the Foundation helps eligible members of our Club community pursue post-secondary education. Scholarships are awarded for one year, and eligible applicants may reapply in later years.

This is a scholarship, not an employee bonus. Applying does not guarantee an interview or an award.

01

Employees: employment must have begun before January 1, 2027, with at least 160 hours worked since the start date.

02

Caddies: at least 15 rounds, preferably 20, in 2026; clearly trending toward that level in 2027.

03

Applicants must be enrolled at a qualified school for Fall 2027. Read the cover letter for the full eligibility requirements.

A LITTLE PREPARATION GOES A LONG WAY

From first step
to finished packet.

Give yourself time to gather documents and request a recommendation. Use this checklist before you submit.

01 / GATHER

Bring the pieces together.

Complete every section and include the required documents. Explain anything that is unavailable; the committee will not follow up to collect missing materials.

Email your digital photo separately to wparks@foxchapelgolfclub.com.

  • ✓Completed current-year application, filled in digitally. Handwritten and prior-year applications are ineligible.
  • ✓One recommendation from someone outside Fox Chapel Golf Club, with their name, address, and telephone number.
  • ✓A personal essay of 1–2 pages about your educational and career goals and how this scholarship would help.
  • ✓First two pages of your FAFSA summary, if submitted. Explain if no FAFSA was submitted.
  • ✓School acceptance or financial-aid offer letter; include an aid estimate if available. Explain unavailable documents.
  • ✓Full-year school costs and scholarship/grant amounts. Do not count loans, work-study, or the Student Aid Index as grant dollars.
  • ✓Applicant signature; parent or guardian signature if under 18.
02 / REVIEW

Check the details.

Use one full academic year and the same school for all costs and aid. Review the totals, signatures, and attachments. Keep original digitally signed documents separate.

03 / DELIVER

Make it official.

Sign and deliver the complete packet as directed in the application. Downloading is only the preparation step.
