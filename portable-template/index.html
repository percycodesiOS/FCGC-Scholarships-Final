AN OPPORTUNITY TO GROW

For the people who
make our Club community.

Promoting personal growth through higher education.

Through the generosity of Club members, the Foundation helps eligible team members pursue post-secondary education. Scholarships are awarded for one year, and eligible applicants may reapply in later years.

This is a scholarship, not an employee bonus. Applying does not guarantee an interview or an award.

Eligibility requirements

  1. 01

    Club employees must have commenced employment before January 1, 2027 and have completed a minimum of 160 hours of work since their start date.

  2. 02

    Caddies must have completed a minimum of 15 rounds (preferably 20) during 2026 and trending toward completing at least 15 rounds (preferably 20) in 2027.

  3. 03

    Applicants must be enrolled at a qualified school for the Fall 2027 semester.
